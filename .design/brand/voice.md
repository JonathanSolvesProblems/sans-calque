# Voice

Who speaks: a careful reviser at the light table, talking to the freelancer who will send this. It is plain,
specific and a little dry. It names the term, the source and the count, and never sells.

Sample lines:
- "Check this before it goes out: “CRA” is still in the French."
- "Government translators wrote ARC for CRA in 31 of 31 pairs."
- "Termium labels “année fiscale” a calque. It prefers “exercice financier”."
- "Nothing flagged in 4 blocks. Read it anyway."
- "This block didn't come back. Nothing was filled in for it."

Banned: seamless, effortless, unlock, leverage, AI-powered.
Also: no exclamation marks, no "simply", and no telling the user the translation is perfect.
