# Found details (Phase 1.6)

The metaphor's world is the tracing table: a lit light table, sheets of tracing vellum, a peg bar, a lamp.
`light-table-tracing-0.jpg` (the rhinoceros) is itself AI-generated. It is excluded from everything.

1. **The lit table** (light-table-tracing-1). Warm ivory in the centre (`#dfd9cf`), falling off to
   sand at the edges. It sits in a bevelled frame with a thin pale lip catching the light.
2. **The desk around it** is dark walnut, nearly black in shadow (`#170f0c`, `#362a22`), with warm
   brown mid-tones (`#71604a`, `#574b3e`).
3. **The peg bar.** A dark steel bar with three pegs (round, slot, round) holds a punched sheet in
   register. The sheet's top strip is darker where it overlaps the bar.
4. **The lamp head** is a flat triangular steel hood on an arm. It casts a hot spot of white light in the
   middle of the sheet.
5. **Frosted vellum is cool** (papier-calque-2/3): blue-grey `#cad1d3`, `#d7e1e6`, `#b3bcbf`. Everything under it is warm.
   The contrast between cool sheet and warm world is in every photo.
6. **Text under vellum** goes soft and loses contrast, but stays readable. Where the sheet curls away, the
   text underneath is suddenly sharp (papier-calque-3). Seen from behind, writing is mirrored.
7. **Saturated colour under vellum desaturates.** The green business card reads pale under the sheet.
8. **Aged tracing paper yellows** to manila (tracing-paper-drawing-3: `#d7b77d`, `#dfbd81`). Its edge
   is torn and uneven. A small round collection stamp sits bottom left, with an accession number in pencil.
9. **Margin notes.** On the ceiling design (tracing-paper-drawing-1) there is handwritten cursive in the
   top and right margins, and a small measuring scale runs up the right edge.
10. **Line quality** (tracing-paper-drawing-0/3): a thin graphite or ink line of uneven weight, with occasional tint
    (green, ochre) laid inside a shape, never as a fill everywhere.
11. **Tape.** The ceiling design's sheet is held by strips of tape that have gone amber at the edges.

Domain facts used with these:
- *Calque* is French for tracing paper. A calque in translation is a word-for-word traced copy, the
  thing the Translation Bureau labels "avoid".
- Animators on a light table correct in coloured pencil over the graphite line.
- Registration (peg bar, punched holes) is what keeps two sheets aligned. That is what this tool checks between
  the English and the French.
