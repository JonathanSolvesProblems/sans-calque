How it was graded

Held-out canada.ca small-business pages, translated by Government of Canada translators. None of these sentences were in training.

What this model is not good at: it learned from government catalogue text, so it is strong on terminology and acronyms and has no particular flair for marketing copy. On held-out government pages its overall quality score is not measurably better than the base model's; the gain is in acronyms and terms. It still gets some acronyms wrong (it writes NIS for SIN, where the government writes NAS), which is why every acronym is checked against the government's own usage. Read the French before you send it, starting with anything flagged.
