Sans calque

English to Canadian French for small-business pages, on a 4-billion-parameter model fine-tuned on government text and small enough to run on a laptop. Every flag below cites where its rule came from.

What this model is not good at: it learned from government catalogue text, so it is strong on terminology and acronyms and has no particular flair for marketing copy. On held-out government pages its overall quality score is not measurably better than the base model's; the gain is in acronyms and terms. It still gets some acronyms wrong (it writes NIS for SIN, where the government writes NAS), which is why every acronym is checked against the government's own usage above. Read the French before you send it, starting with anything flagged.
